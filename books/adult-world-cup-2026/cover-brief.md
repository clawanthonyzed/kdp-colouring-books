# Cover Design Brief — World Cup 2026 Adult Colouring Book

## Canva Template

Use: KDP Book Cover template (8.5" × 11" front cover + bleed)
Canvas size: 8.625" × 11.25" (with bleed)
Resolution: 300 DPI
Color mode: CMYK

---

## Design Direction

**Mood:** Premium, sophisticated, eye-catching. Stands out in the Amazon Books category.
**Style:** Art Deco / Mandala fusion. Gold, deep navy, white.
**Primary hook:** "2026 WORLD CUP" must be immediately readable as a thumbnail.

---

## Cover Layers (build in this order in Canva)

### Layer 1 — Background
- Deep navy blue (#0A1628) full bleed background
- Subtle mandala pattern in slightly lighter navy (10% opacity) — use a Canva mandala element or geometric repeat pattern

### Layer 2 — Hero Image (centre)
- The FIFA World Cup trophy illustration (use a free SVG or Canva element, or use the cover art generated by brush/Leonardo)
- Style: gold line art version of the trophy on the navy background
- Trophy position: centred, taking up 50% of the cover height
- Trophy colour: Gold gradient (#D4AF37 to #F5D67B)

### Layer 3 — Mandala Surround
- Circular mandala pattern radiating from behind the trophy
- Colour: Gold (#D4AF37) at low opacity (30-40%) so it's subtle
- Use Canva's mandala or geometric burst element

### Layer 4 — Typography

**Main Title:**
```
2026 FIFA
WORLD CUP
```
- Font: Cinzel (serif, caps) or Playfair Display
- Size: Large — must be readable at thumbnail
- Colour: Bright gold (#F5D67B)
- Position: Upper portion of cover, above trophy

**Subtitle:**
```
ADULT COLORING BOOK
```
- Font: Same family, lighter weight
- Size: Medium
- Colour: White
- Position: Between title and trophy

**Page count + hook:**
```
40 INTRICATE DESIGNS
Mandalas · Art Nouveau · Geometric
```
- Font: Clean sans-serif (Montserrat or similar)
- Size: Small
- Colour: White at 80% opacity
- Position: Below trophy

**Publisher:**
```
Cudan Studio
```
- Small, bottom of cover
- White

### Layer 5 — Decorative Elements
- Top corners: Small Art Deco geometric corner ornaments in gold
- Bottom strip: Subtle striped gold band
- Around trophy: Small soccer ball hexagon motifs scattered
- Side borders: Thin gold lines framing the composition

---

## Thumbnail Test

Before finalising, shrink the design to 160px wide (Amazon thumbnail size).
Check: Is "WORLD CUP 2026" readable? Is the trophy visible? Is it eye-catching vs competitors?

---

## Key Competitor Colours to AVOID (stand out from them)

- Plain black and white covers — ours has gold + navy
- Red/white football imagery — ours is premium Art Deco
- Plain typography-only covers — ours has strong central imagery

---

## Spine Design

Width: 0.194" (86 pages at 60gsm)
- Repeat the title vertically in small gold text on navy spine
- Small trophy icon at spine top

## Back Cover

- Brief description (3-4 lines from listing)
- Barcode placeholder (bottom right)
- Publisher logo area (bottom centre)
- 1-2 preview interior page thumbnails (top area)
- "Printed in the USA" text (KDP requirement)
