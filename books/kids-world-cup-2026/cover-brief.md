# Cover Design Brief — World Cup 2026 Kids Colouring Book

## Canva Template

Use: KDP Book Cover template (8.5" × 11" front cover + bleed)
Canvas size: 8.625" × 11.25" (with bleed)
Resolution: 300 DPI
Color mode: CMYK

---

## Design Direction

**Mood:** Energetic, fun, colourful, instantly appealing to children AND parents browsing Amazon.
**Style:** Bold cartoon/comic style. Bright primary colours. Maximum energy.
**Primary hook:** Big fun character in the centre + "WORLD CUP 2026" instantly readable.

---

## Cover Layers (build in this order in Canva)

### Layer 1 — Background
- Bright sky blue background (#1E90FF)
- White cloud shapes scattered around edges (Canva cloud elements)
- Subtle soccer hexagon pattern at very low opacity (10%) in slightly darker blue

### Layer 2 — Hero Character (centre)
- Large cartoon soccer ball character with a huge grin, big eyes, wearing a national jersey
- OR: A group of 3-4 fun animal players (penguin, lion, dinosaur) in action
- Position: Dominant, taking up 60% of the cover
- Style: Bold black outlines, large colour areas — matches the interior style
- Background action: Stars, sparkles, and confetti exploding around the character

### Layer 3 — Typography

**Main Title:**
```
WORLD CUP 2026
```
- Font: Fredoka One or Nunito (rounded, fun, bold)
- Size: HUGE — top of the cover, must dominate
- Colour: Bright yellow (#FFD700) with bold black outline
- Effect: Slight drop shadow or outline for readability

**Subtitle:**
```
KIDS COLORING BOOK
```
- Font: Same rounded family
- Size: Large
- Colour: White with black outline
- Position: Just below main title

**Age indicator:**
```
Ages 4-12
```
- Font: Bold sans-serif in rounded style
- Size: Medium
- Colour: Bright orange circle badge
- Position: Top corner badge

**Hook line:**
```
40 FUN SOCCER ADVENTURES!
```
- Font: Bold playful font
- Size: Medium
- Colour: Bright red or orange on a banner element
- Position: Below the hero character, above bottom

### Layer 4 — Decorative Elements
- Stars and sparkles scattered across cover (Canva sparkle elements)
- Confetti shapes in multiple bright colours (yellow, orange, green, red)
- Small soccer ball motifs scattered around edges
- Trophy icon in top corner area (small, gold)
- Flags of different nations as small decorations

### Layer 5 — Energy Lines
- Speed/action lines radiating from the central character
- Radiating star burst behind the main character for maximum energy

## Competitor Analysis — Stand Out From:

- Boring plain text covers
- Low-energy single-colour backgrounds
- Adult-looking designs marketed as kids books
- Poor quality character illustrations

Our differentiators: Maximum fun energy, professional quality characters, bold readable title, bright multi-colour palette.
