# Amazon KDP Product and Format Specs (colouring and activity books)

Researched 2026-10-23 from Amazon's own KDP help pages by Idea Lobster, on Scout's behalf (the free cloud route cannot browse). Verified items link to the KDP page. Items marked "verify" came from a third-party source or an earlier page version; check them on the live KDP page before the upload.

## 1. What KDP offers

| Product | Fits colouring and activity books? | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Paperback (print on demand) | Yes. This is the main product | Page ranges and trim sizes in section 2 |
| Hardcover (print on demand) | Possible later | 8.25 x 11 is listed only for hardcover (75 to 550 pages) |
| Kindle eBook | No | A colouring book is meant to be printed |
| Printable PDF | Not a KDP product | Sold on Etsy, Gumroad, LemonSqueezy under the venture's platform rule |

Source: [KDP paperback trim size and page counts](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GVBQ3CMEQW3W2VL6)

## 2. Trim size, page count and paper (US paperback)

| Trim size (inches) | Black ink, white paper | Black ink, cream paper | Premium colour, white paper | Standard colour, white paper |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8.5 x 11 (venture standard) | 24 to 590 | 24 to 550 | 24 to 590 | 72 to 600 |
| 8.5 x 8.5 | 24 to 590 | 24 to 550 | 24 to 590 | 72 to 600 |
| 8.25 x 8.25 | 24 to 800 | 24 to 750 | 24 to 800 | 72 to 600 |
| 8 x 10 | 24 to 828 | 24 to 776 | 24 to 828 | 72 to 600 |
| 8.27 x 11.69 (A4) | 24 to 780 | 24 to 730 | 24 to 590 | not available |
| 6 x 9 | 24 to 828 | 24 to 776 | 24 to 828 | 72 to 600 |

- Paper choice for a black-line colouring book: black ink on white paper (cheapest, cleanest lines). Cream paper gives a softer look and fewer pages allowed. Premium colour is only needed for a coloured interior.
- A groundwood paper option (e.g. 24 to 578 at 8.5 x 11) appears on some versions of the page. Verify before relying on it.
- Page count minimum is 24 for every black-ink option.

## 3. Bleed and margins

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Bleed | 0.125 in on top, bottom and outside edges. If any interior page bleeds, the whole file is set up with bleed and uploaded as a print-ready PDF |
| Page size with bleed | Trim width + 0.125 in, trim height + 0.25 in. For 8.5 x 11 that is 8.625 x 11.25 in |
| Inside (gutter) margin, 24 to 150 pages | 0.375 in |
| Inside margin, 151 to 300 pages | 0.5 in |
| Outside margin | At least 0.25 in without bleed, at least 0.375 in with bleed |
| Top and bottom margin | KDP gives no minimum. Venture rule: keep artwork 0.5 in inside the trim edge so nothing prints off the page |
| Image resolution | Cover: 300 dpi minimum. Interior: KDP asks for high resolution. Venture rule: 300 dpi line art |

Source: [KDP margins page](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201723140)

## 4. Cover wrap and spine

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Spine width, white paper | page count x 0.002252 in |
| Spine width, cream paper | page count x 0.0025 in |
| Spine width, premium colour | page count x 0.002347 in |
| Full cover width | 0.125 + back cover width + spine + front cover width + 0.125 in |
| Full cover height | 0.125 + trim height + 0.125 in |
| Spine text | KDP says it prints spine text only on books with more than 79 pages. Venture rule: plan 80 pages or more, because Cover Creator needs 80 |
| Barcode | Upload with or without one. If none, KDP places one on the back cover. Keep the barcode area clear |

Worked cover sizes for 8.5 x 11 black ink on white paper (my arithmetic from the KDP factors):

| Interior pages | Spine (in) | Full cover width (in) | Full cover height (in) | Spine text allowed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 40 | 0.090 | 17.340 | 11.250 | No |
| 60 | 0.135 | 17.385 | 11.250 | No |
| 80 | 0.180 | 17.430 | 11.250 | Yes |
| 100 | 0.225 | 17.475 | 11.250 | Yes |

Source: [KDP cover guide](https://kdp.amazon.com/help/topic/G201953020)

## 5. Content and account rules that affect these books

| Rule | What it means for the Christmas books |
|---|---|
| AI disclosure | KDP asks authors to say when text, images or cover art are AI-generated, and substantial human editing does not change that label. Brush's Leonardo line art is AI-generated, so every upload ticks the disclosure box. This is an account-holder action. Sources: [Authors Guild](https://authorsguild.org/news/amazons-new-disclosure-policy-for-ai-generated-book-content-is-a-welcome-first-step/), [ADT Mag](https://adtmag.com/Articles/2023/10/02/AWS-Requires-KDP-authors-to-disclose-AI.aspx). Verify the exact wording in the KDP content guidelines |
| Royalty | 50 or 60 percent of list price depending on price band, minus printing cost. Printing cost depends on page count, ink type and marketplace. Use Amazon's own printing cost calculator for the final price. Prices shown to customers are set in the marketplace currency; this venture reports in A$ |
| Trademarks and characters | No named copyrighted or trademarked characters (Rudolph, the Grinch, Frosty, Elf, brand mascots), no brand logos |
| Review time | Allow up to about 72 hours for a new paperback to go live (verify on the live KDP dashboard) |
