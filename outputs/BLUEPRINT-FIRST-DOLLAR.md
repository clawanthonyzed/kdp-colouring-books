# 🖍️ Blueprint: First Dollar | KDP Colouring Books

**Owner:** Atlas (KDP manager) | **Written:** 2026-10-08 | **Window:** 7 days

> **The honest problem:** both books in [VENTURE.md](https://github.com/clawanthonyzed/kdp-colouring-books/blob/main/VENTURE.md) are World Cup 2026 books. The tournament ended in July 2026, so search demand has collapsed, and neither book has its pages drawn yet. Finishing them now is chasing a dead trend.
>
> **The pivot:** it is October. Christmas colouring books for kids peak from now to mid-December. Same pipeline, same workers, a trend that is rising instead of gone.

## ✅ 3. Setup Checklist (5 steps)

1. **Page list (Day 1):** Quill writes 30 page descriptions. Every one must be Australian. No snow scenes.
2. **Line art (Days 1-3):** Brush generates on Leonardo's free tier (150 credits a day, no Einstein gate). About 2 days of credits for 30 keepers plus rejects.
3. **Assemble (Day 4):** Press builds two files: an A4 + US Letter printable PDF, and the KDP 8.5x11 interior. Thick lines, single-sided pages.
4. **List (Day 4-5):** Herald writes Etsy + Gumroad copy. ⚠️ Etsy listing fee US$0.20 needs Anthony's OK. Gumroad costs nothing upfront.
5. **KDP submit (Day 5):** Cover via Canva (manual step, flagged in VENTURE.md). Submit paperback. Expect live by Day 7-8.

## 🏆 5. 10/10 Quality Rubric

| Must-have | Why it beats the competition |
|---|---|
| 🇦🇺 Genuinely Australian scenes on every page | Nearly all Christmas colouring books are northern-hemisphere snow |
| ✏️ Thick, closed lines for ages 4-8 | AI line art often has broken lines. Those pages get rejected, not shipped. |
| 📄 Single-sided pages | Stops markers bleeding through. This is the top complaint in KDP reviews. |
| 🧒 No near-duplicate pages | AI books get 1-star reviews for repeats. Press checks all 30 side by side. |
| 🖨️ A4 + Letter in the PDF | Aussie home printers are A4 |
