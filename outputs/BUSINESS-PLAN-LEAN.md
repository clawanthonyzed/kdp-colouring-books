## 🧮 3. Break-Even Math

Fixed costs: **A$0.** Leonardo free tier (150 credits/day), Canva free, Gumroad free, KDP takes print cost from royalties (no upfront cost).

| Line | PDF A$6.90 (Gumroad) | Paperback A$12.99 (KDP) |
|---|---|---|
| Platform cut | 10% = A$0.69 + US$0.50 (A$0.72) | 40% = A$5.20 (KDP keeps 40%) |
| Print cost | none | **estimate A$3.30** (US$2.30 US-marketplace rate, converted). Confirm in KDP calculator |
| **Net per sale** | **A$5.49** | **about A$4.49** |

| Goal | Sales per month (mixed PDF + paperback, about A$5 each) |
|---|---|
| 🥇 First dollar | 1 |
| A$500/month | about 100 (3 a day) across the catalogue |
| **A$2,000/month** | **about 400** (13 a day). Only reachable with a catalogue of 12 to 15 books |

Honest read: colouring books are a **volume + catalogue** game. One book will not reach target; the factory will.
