# Playbook: KDP upload handoff (KDP Colouring Books, venture #38)

Written 2026-10-24 under the Autonomous Playbook Protocol. Owner: Atlas. Answers Anthony's question "Will the agents be able to upload to KDP without my approval?": no, by design.

## Hard stops
- No agent holds, stores or asks for Amazon or KDP credentials.
- No agent uploads, prices, publishes, or presses any button in the KDP dashboard. The upload is done by Anthony in his own account.
- The AI-disclosure answers (text, images, translations) are given by the account holder. For this series the answer is yes to all three.
- Browser-automation bots that log in to KDP are not used: their compliance with KDP's terms is unclear and they would need the credentials.

## Handoff steps
| # | Owner | Step | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Atlas | Final files in the private second_brain folder: interior PDF, full-wrap cover PDF, QC report | Links open |
| 2 | Atlas | Listing sheet per marketplace: title, subtitle, 7 keywords, 2 categories, description | Sheet on /tasks |
| 3 | Idea Lobster | Review card on /tasks with links, price options and open risks | Card id in the report |
| 4 | Anthony | Approve the card | Status completed |
| 5 | Anthony | In KDP: Create paperback, paste the listing, choose ink (Premium colour), trim 8.5 x 11, upload interior and cover, answer the AI questions, use the previewer, set price and territories | Previewer shows no errors |
| 6 | Anthony | Click Publish. KDP reviews for up to about 72 hours | Book shows In review |

## Escalation
Only money, accounts, legal and owner-only facts go to Anthony: price, ink choice, imprint, account, AI-disclosure answer, native reviewer budget.
