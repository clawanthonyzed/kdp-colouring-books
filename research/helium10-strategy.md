# Helium 10 Research Strategy — KDP Colouring Books

## Objective

Use Helium 10 to validate and refine the World Cup 2026 niche. Find additional profitable niches for future books. Optimise keyword strategy for maximum Amazon rank.

---

## Tool-by-Tool Workflow

### 1. Magnet — Keyword Volume Research

**Seed keywords to run:**
```
world cup 2026 coloring book
soccer coloring book adults
football coloring book 2026
world cup activity book
soccer coloring book children
football adult coloring
FIFA 2026 book
world cup 2026 gift
soccer mandala coloring
football colouring book UK
```

**What to capture per keyword:**
- Search volume (monthly)
- Trend (seasonal spike direction)
- Competition score
- Match type suggestions

**Target thresholds:**
- Volume > 1,000/month = viable
- Volume > 10,000/month = high priority
- Competition score < 70 = opportunity

---

### 2. Black Box — Niche Discovery

**Settings for coloring books:**
```
Category: Books
Sub-category: Arts & Photography > Drawing
Min Price: $8
Max Reviews: 100
Min BSR: 1
Max BSR: 100,000
Min Rating: 4.0
```

**What to look for:**
- Books with BSR < 50,000 and Reviews < 30 (recent winners)
- Seasonal topics about to peak (World Cup is June-July 2026)
- Under-served niches with low competition

---

### 3. Cerebro — Competitor Reverse ASIN

**Pull ASINs for top 5 competitor coloring books:**
1. Search "world cup coloring book" on Amazon
2. Note top 5 ASINs that are actually selling (BSR < 100,000)
3. Run each ASIN through Cerebro

**What to extract:**
- All keywords these books rank for
- Keywords they rank #1-10 for
- Keywords with > 500 searches they DON'T rank for (opportunity gaps)

---

### 4. Frankenstein — Keyword Optimisation

**Input:** All keyword lists from Magnet + Cerebro
**Output:** Deduplicated, frequency-sorted master list
**Use for:** Backend keyword fields in KDP (7 × 50 char fields)
